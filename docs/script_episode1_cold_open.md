# Episode 1 — Cold Open ("Green")
~5 minutes. Provided by Chris 2026-09-13, pasted directly in chat (no other copy existed on
disk, GitHub, Google Drive, or Notion — all four were searched before this arrived).

FADE IN:

EXT. SUBURBAN STREET — NIGHT — RAIN

Quiet. Wide, still. A residential street of large, modern houses — solar roofs, manicured hedges, a monorail line passing silently on stilts in the far background. Beyond the rooftops, the real skyline: dense neon, kilometers of it, throwing pink and cyan light low across the clouds. The suburb is dark and calm by contrast. Safe. Bought.

Rain ticks on car roofs. A single automated delivery drone hums past overhead, unremarkable, gone.

We push slowly toward one house — warmer-lit than its neighbors, windows glowing gold against the wet dark.

CUT TO:

INT. SMITH HOUSE — KITCHEN — CONTINUOUS

IRIS stands at the counter, drying a plate by hand. Unhurried. Precise without looking mechanical. A thin green light glows steady on her forehead.

She sets the plate down, notices a child's inhaler left on the counter, moves it to a drawer clearly meant for it. A small, practiced act of care. Nobody asked her to.

Through the window behind her: the neon skyline, distant, glimmering in the rain.

JAMES SMITH enters, pouring water from the tap rather than asking her to.

JAMES
Sierra still awake?

IRIS
Reading. I told her lights out in ten.

JAMES
She'll say twenty.

IRIS
I said ten.

James lingers, glances toward the window and the city glow, then away.

JAMES
Good night for staying in.

He exits. Iris watches him go. The green light doesn't change.

CUT TO:

INT. SMITH HOUSE — UPSTAIRS HALLWAY — MOMENTS LATER

SIERRA SMITH (12) is under the covers with a tablet.

IRIS
Ten minutes was generous.

SIERRA
It's educational.

IRIS
It's a racing game.

SIERRA
Educational racing game.

Iris takes the tablet, sets it down. No argument — this has happened a hundred times before.

SIERRA
Do you sleep?

IRIS
No.

SIERRA
Ever get bored?

IRIS
Not yet.

CUT TO:

INT. SMITH HOUSE — JACOB'S ROOM — CONTINUOUS

JACOB SMITH (10) is already half-asleep. Iris straightens his blanket, pauses to watch him breathe, turns off the lamp.

CUT TO:

INT. SMITH HOUSE — KITCHEN — LATER

The house has gone quiet. Iris wipes the counter alone. Rain against the window. Green light steady.

She pauses. Head tilting a few degrees. Outside, faint: a car door. Then another. Voices, too clipped and fast for idle chat.

She turns fully toward the window. The forehead light shifts — green fading, replaced by a slow-rising amber, the first time we've seen it change.

She doesn't move yet. Just watches.

HOLD ON HER FACE.

CUT TO BLACK.

END OF COLD OPEN.
